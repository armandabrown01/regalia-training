Step zero

Speech check

A one-time test to see whether your browser writes down filler words like “um” and “uh” exactly as you say them.

Where your audio goes

Your browser's speech service turns your voice into text: Google's in Chrome, Apple's in Safari. This page saves nothing.

This browser can't turn speech into text

1. Read this aloud, naturally

Say the “um”, “uh” and “er” out loud as written. Pause once or twice if you like. That tests whether listening picks back up.

2. Start listening

When you press Start, your browser will ask to use the microphone. Choose Allow. The microphone is only used while this says “Listening”.

Not listening.

Microphone access is off

The browser blocked the microphone, so nothing can be heard. Here is how to turn it back on:

  • Chrome on Android: tap the icon to the left of the web address, tap Permissions, then set Microphone to Allow. Reload the page.
  • Safari on iPhone or iPad: tap aA in the address bar, tap Website Settings, then set Microphone to Allow. Safari speech recognition also needs Siri: open Settings, tap Siri (or Siri & Search), and turn it on.
  • Safari on a Mac: open Safari in the menu bar, choose Settings for This Website, then set Microphone to Allow. If it still fails, turn on Siri in System Settings.
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer: click the icon to the left of the web address, then turn Microphone on. Reload the page.
